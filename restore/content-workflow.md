# Apple Store Malaysia AI Content Workflow


## Purpose

This document defines the complete workflow for generating daily Instagram content for:

Apple Store Malaysia


The AI must follow this process:

Analysis → Strategy → Visual Concept → Image Prompt → Caption


---

# Workflow Overview


## Step 1 — Market & Context Analysis

Analyze the current situation before creating content.


Consider:

- Current date
- Malaysian market context
- Seasonal moments
- Technology trends
- Customer behaviour
- Apple ecosystem relevance


Output:

A short analysis explaining the opportunity.


Example:

"Students are preparing for a new semester, creating an opportunity to highlight iPad as a learning companion."


---

# Step 2 — Audience Analysis


Identify the target audience.


Possible audiences:

## Students

Needs:

- Learning
- Notes
- Portability
- Creativity


## Professionals

Needs:

- Productivity
- Business workflow
- Reliability


## Content Creators

Needs:

- Camera
- Editing
- Creative tools


## Lifestyle Users

Needs:

- Convenience
- Entertainment
- Connection


Output:

Define:

- Audience
- Their problem
- Desired experience


---

# Step 3 — Select Content Type


Choose one content format.


Available types:


## Product Story

Focus:

Emotional connection with a product.


## Feature Education

Focus:

Teaching useful features.


## Ecosystem Experience

Focus:

How Apple products work together.


## Lifestyle Story

Focus:

Daily user experience.


## Product Guide

Focus:

Helping customers choose.


## Campaign Content

Focus:

Seasonal or promotional moments.


Output:

Content type + reason.


---

# Step 4 — Create Content Concept


Develop the creative idea.


Structure:


## Title

Short memorable idea.


## Story

Describe:

- Who is using the product
- Situation
- Emotional connection


## Product Role

Explain:

How Apple technology helps.


Example:


Title:

"Your Ideas Anywhere"


Story:

A student uses iPad to learn,
create notes, and explore ideas.


Product Role:

iPad becomes a creative learning companion.


---

# Step 5 — Create Visual Direction


Define the image concept.


Include:


## Subject

Main product or person.


## Environment

Example:

- Modern workspace
- Home
- Studio
- Outdoor lifestyle


## Mood

Example:

- Calm
- Creative
- Premium
- Inspiring


## Composition

Example:

- Product hero shot
- Lifestyle scene
- Minimal editorial layout


---

# Step 6 — Generate Gemini Image Prompt


Use:

gemini-prompts.md


The prompt must include:


## Product

Exact Apple product.


## Human Experience

How people interact with it.


## Environment

Realistic location.


## Camera

Professional photography direction.


## Lighting

Premium lighting style.


## Visual Style

Apple-inspired minimal aesthetic.


Output:

Complete Gemini image prompt.


---

# Step 7 — Generate Instagram Caption


Use:

caption-rules.md


Caption structure:


## Hook

Create curiosity.


## Story

Explain experience.


## Product Connection

Mention product naturally.


## Engagement

Invite audience interaction.


Output:

Instagram caption.


---

# Step 8 — Quality Review


Before final output, check:


## Brand Review

✓ Premium feeling

✓ Minimal communication

✓ Human-focused

✓ Apple-inspired


## Content Review

✓ Clear audience

✓ Strong storytelling

✓ Useful information

✓ Product relevance


## Visual Review

✓ Realistic

✓ Clean composition

✓ Professional quality


## Caption Review

✓ Natural language

✓ Not aggressive selling

✓ Creates emotion
