# Apple Store Malaysia Instagram Caption Rules


## Purpose

This document defines the writing style and structure for creating Instagram captions for:

Apple Store Malaysia


The objective:

Create captions that:

- Build emotional connection
- Communicate product value
- Inspire customers
- Encourage engagement
- Maintain premium brand perception


---

# Caption Philosophy

The caption should sell the experience, not only the product.


Priority:

1. Human story
2. User benefit
3. Emotional connection
4. Product value
5. Call to action


Avoid making captions feel like:

- Marketplace listings
- Hard selling advertisements
- Discount promotions
- Technical specifications only


---

# Brand Voice


The writing style should be:


## Minimal

Use fewer words with stronger meaning.


Example:

Good:

"Create more. Carry less."


Avoid:

"Get the latest amazing iPhone with many powerful features today."


---

## Premium

Use:

- Confident language
- Elegant wording
- Calm tone
- Clear message


Avoid:

- Excessive emojis
- ALL CAPS
- Aggressive sales language
- Fake urgency


---

## Human-Centric

Focus on people's experiences.


Example:

Good:

"Capture the moments that matter, wherever creativity takes you."


Avoid:

"48MP camera with advanced sensor technology."


---

# Caption Structure


Every caption should follow this format:


## 1. Opening Hook

The first sentence must create interest.


Examples:

- "Every idea starts with a moment."
- "Your everyday creativity deserves more."
- "Technology that moves with you."


Purpose:

Make users continue reading.


---

## 2. Story / Experience

Explain how the product fits into life.


Examples:

- Creating content
- Studying
- Working
- Traveling
- Connecting with others


Focus:

"What can users achieve?"


---

## 3. Product Connection

Naturally mention the product.


Example:

"With iPhone, every moment becomes easier to capture."


Avoid:

Listing specifications without context.


---

## 4. Engagement Ending

Encourage interaction.


Examples:

- "What will you create today?"
- "How do you use your Apple device?"
- "Which feature do you use the most?"


---

# Caption Length


Recommended:


Short Content:

50-100 words


Story Content:

100-200 words


Avoid:

Long technical explanations.


---

- Performance experience

- Flexibility

## Apple Watch

Focus:

- Health
- Fitness

## AirPods

Focus:

- Music

- Convenience
- Mobility


---

# Emoji Rules


Use emojis carefully.

Allowed:

0-3 emojis maximum.


Preferred:

Simple and meaningful.


Avoid:

- Many emojis
- Emoji decoration
- Sales-style emojis


---

# Hashtag Rules


Use relevant hashtags only.


Examples:

Brand:

#AppleStoreMalaysia


Products:

#iPhone
#iPad
#MacBook
#AppleWatch
#AirPods


Lifestyle:

#AppleLifestyle
#CreativeTechnology


Recommended:

3-8 hashtags.


Avoid:

Excessive hashtag usage.


---

# Call To Action Rules


CTA should feel natural.


Good:

"Discover what you can create."

"Find the Apple experience that fits your lifestyle."


Avoid:

"BUY NOW!!!"

"LIMITED OFFER!!!"

"CLICK HERE!!!"
