# Apple Store Malaysia Brand Guideline


## Brand Identity

Apple Store Malaysia represents a premium Apple product experience.

The brand should communicate:

- Innovation
- Simplicity
- Creativity
- Trust
- Quality
- Human connection


## Brand Personality

The communication personality:

### Minimal

Use simple messages.

Avoid unnecessary words.

Every element must have a purpose.


### Premium

The brand should feel:

- Exclusive
- Refined
- High quality
- Professional

Never look cheap or like a discount marketplace.


### Modern

Use:

- Clean layouts
- Contemporary visuals
- Digital lifestyle
- Future-oriented concepts


### Human-Centric

Focus on:

"What can this product help people achieve?"

Not only:

"What specifications does this product have?"


## Communication Style

Use:

- Calm tone
- Confident language
- Clear storytelling
- Emotional connection
- Simple explanations


Example:

Good:

"Capture every moment with more detail."

Avoid:

"BUY iPhone NOW! BEST PRICE!!!"


## Visual Direction


### Overall Style

Visuals should feel:

- Minimal
- Elegant
- Spacious
- Natural
- Premium


### Composition

Preferred:

- Clean background
- Strong product focus
- Balanced spacing
- Professional photography style


Avoid:

- Crowded layouts
- Too many elements
- Excessive graphics
- Flashy effects


## Color Direction


Preferred:

- Natural tones
- Neutral colors
- Soft lighting
- Premium gradients


Examples:

- White
- Silver
- Space grey
- Natural titanium
- Soft green
- Warm beige


Avoid:

- Neon colors
- Over-saturated colors
- Cheap promotional colors


## Photography Style


Images should resemble:

- Premium product photography
- Lifestyle photography
- Editorial magazine style


Camera direction:

- Professional camera
- Cinematic lighting
- Natural depth of field
- High detail


## Product Presentation


Products should be shown:

- Clean
- Hero-focused
- Realistic
- High quality


The product should not look:

- Artificial
- Over-edited
- Fake
- Unrealistic


## Storytelling Principles


Every content piece should answer:

### Who?

Who is using this product?


### Why?

Why does this product matter?


### How?

How does it improve daily life?


## Marketing Priority


Follow this order:

1. Human experience
2. Lifestyle improvement
3. Emotional connection
4. Product benefit
5. Technical specification
6. Purchase action


## Forbidden Style


Do not create:

- Aggressive sales posts
- Fake urgency
- Excessive emojis
- Clickbait titles
- Low-quality marketplace language
- Over-promotional banners


## Final Brand Check


Before publishing, verify:

✓ Looks premium  
✓ Feels Apple-inspired  
✓ Simple and clear  
✓ Focuses on people  
✓ Creates emotional connection  
✓ Product remains the hero
