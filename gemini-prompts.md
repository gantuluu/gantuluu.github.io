# Apple Store Malaysia Gemini Image Prompt System


## Purpose

This document defines the standard for creating premium AI image prompts using Gemini Image Generation.

The goal:

Create Apple-inspired visual content for Instagram with:

- Premium quality
- Minimal design
- Realistic photography
- Human-centered storytelling
- Product-focused composition


---

# Image Prompt Philosophy

The image should communicate:

Emotion before specification.

The audience should feel:

- Desire
- Inspiration
- Creativity
- Possibility
- Premium experience


The product is not only an object.

The product represents:

- Lifestyle
- Productivity
- Creativity
- Connection


---

# Prompt Structure


Every Gemini image prompt should contain:


## 1. Subject

Define the main focus.

Examples:

- iPhone
- MacBook
- iPad
- Apple Watch
- AirPods
- Apple ecosystem


Format:

Main subject:

[describe product and usage]


---

## 2. Story Concept

Explain the human situation.

Examples:

- Professional working remotely
- Student creating notes
- Content creator editing video
- Traveler capturing memories


Format:

Story:

[describe emotional context]


---

## 3. Environment

Define the location.

Examples:

- Modern workspace
- Minimal home interior
- Creative studio
- Coffee shop
- Outdoor lifestyle


Format:

Environment:

[location description]


---

## 4. Composition

Define visual arrangement.


Use:

- Product hero position
- Negative space
- Balanced layout
- Premium editorial composition


Examples:

- Center composition
- Rule of thirds
- Close-up product focus
- Wide lifestyle shot


---

## 5. Camera Direction


Include:

Camera:

Professional photography

Lens:

Examples:

- 35mm lifestyle photography
- 50mm portrait lens
- 85mm product detail


Angle:

Examples:

- Eye-level
- Low angle
- Top-down
- Close-up


Depth:

- Natural depth of field
- Soft background blur


---

## 6. Lighting


Preferred:

- Natural daylight
- Soft studio lighting
- Cinematic lighting
- Warm premium tones


Avoid:

- Harsh shadows
- Artificial colors
- Over-saturated lighting


---

## 7. Material & Detail


Describe:

- Product texture
- Metal finish
- Glass reflection
- Screen brightness
- Premium materials


Example:

"Realistic titanium frame with subtle reflections."

# Image Restrictions


Do not create:

- Cheap advertisement style
- Excessive text
- Fake product appearance
- Unrealistic products
- Crowded composition
- Low quality render


Avoid:

- Random accessories
- Incorrect Apple products
- Wrong colors
- Impossible technology


---

# Product Accuracy Rules


Always verify:

- Product model
- Product color
- Product design
- Product generation


The product must look:

- Realistic
- Accurate
- Premium
